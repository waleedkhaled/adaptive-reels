# UX specification

Written 2026-10-04 against `docs/PLAN.md`, `docs/research/01` to `11`, `DESIGN.md` (repo root), the draft `PRODUCT.md` and `docs/PERFORMANCE.md`; revised the same day after review. `DESIGN.md` owns tokens, sizes, marks and fit formulas, `PERFORMANCE.md` the list and player mechanics; this file owns behaviour, flows, copy, accessibility and the demo. Sizes are pt on iOS and dp on Android. **Geometry is computed and text fit is estimated; nothing has run on a phone, and the agent is not built, so the demo sequence is unsimulated.** OWNER marks a decision listed in section 10.

## 1. Principles

1. **Watching is not progress.** A watched reel adds a grey dot and nothing else: no colour, haptic or panel, and no counter in the feed. Only answers fill a tile; Learned needs a correct answer on or after `dueAt`. Evidence: re-reading loses to testing at 1 week (42% vs 56%) and learners predict the opposite (research 03, finding 1).
2. **Say the true reason, and make every control visibly change the next item.** One reason sentence per item, from the agent's reason code; a quick action names the real next item and offers Undo. Evidence: 39% of surveyed YouTube users said its feed controls had no effect (03, finding 9).
3. **No baked text is cropped or covered; chrome is solid, computed, and sits only over the reel's flat bands.** Evidence: overlays land on captions (03, finding 13); bands must come from insets, not one phone size (04, fact-check).
4. **Show a change when it happens, at a readable size, then once more as one event on the map.** Evidence: three minutes yield about 4 Seen, 1 to 2 Checked and 1 Learned of 40, so the beat is the 6-concept unlock, not shading (01 and 02, fact-checks).
5. **Nothing hides behind a long-press or double-tap, nothing is told by colour, a timer or motion alone, and the session may end.** Evidence: WCAG 1.4.1, 2.5.1, 2.2.1, 2.2.2, 1.4.2 (03, finding 14); a habitual swipe must not skip a check, so the pager locks on cards (03, fact-check).

## 2. Information architecture and navigation

| Route (`src/app/`)    | Kind                             | Tab bar | Purpose                                                                                 |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `_layout`             | root Stack                       | -       | Holds the splash until fonts, profile and manifest are ready; no profile: `onboarding`. |
| `onboarding`          | Stack screen (replaced, no back) | no      | Topic picker. First launch and after Reset.                                             |
| `(tabs)/index`        | tab: **Feed**                    | yes     | One vertical pager: reels, retrieval cards, summary items.                              |
| `(tabs)/you`          | tab: **You**                     | yes     | Settings (3.8).                                                                         |
| `map`                 | **pushed** Stack screen          | no      | Knowledge map (Matrix + Rail + dock).                                                   |
| `session/[conceptId]` | **pushed** Stack screen          | no      | Gap session or a one-card map review, on the feed's pager and players.                  |

- **Tab bar:** Feed, Map, You. Feed and You switch tabs. **Map pushes a full-height screen** with a back control: under a 48 pt bar the eight-row replay does not fit on phones up to 393x852. OWNER.
- **Map entry points:** the Map item, the progress panel, "Open map" on a summary item. The Map item shows an 8 pt square "changed" mark after an answer changed a level; never after watching.
- **In-screen panels, never routes or native sheets:** reason (240 pt), Key (320, scrolls), concept and dock detail (340), confirmations. A tap outside, "Close" or Android back closes the top one.
- **Light / Focus:** You only; Light on first launch. OWNER.
- **Demo clock:** You only. On: stores the current mode, sets Focus (the loop cannot close in Light; 03 fact-check) and says so. Off: restores the stored mode. Either switch rescales every pending `dueAt` to the new interval table, counted from that concept's last answer. While on, the reason panel adds "Demo clock: reviews return in about 1 min." OWNER.
- **Leaving the feed** (Map, You, session, background under 30 min) pauses the players and the open impression and never ends it: returning resumes the reel where it was, and watch signals are computed once, when the learner swipes away. Only a session that ends while away closes it: `ended_by: blur`, classed Partial (no interest change; exposure by the usual position rule).
- **Android back** closes a panel, else pops `map` or asks before leaving `session`, else leaves the app from Feed.

### 3.2 Feed: reel cell

- **Top row.** Running head on the feed axis: the concept's 18 pt state tile (state at impression time), the concept, " in {topic short name}"; one line, topic truncates first. Tap opens the **concept panel**: summary from `graph.json` and the scalable **transcript**. Right: Sound control, "Sound off" / "Sound on".
- **Picture.** Contain-fit. Tap toggles pause; the paused mark (play icon, "Paused") is the Play button. A reel loops and never advances by itself; after 2 loops with no input it pauses (at most one rewatch signal per impression).
- **Progress.** The 2 pt line on the strip's top edge; the picture's accessibility value carries "12 of 29 seconds".
- **Reason strip** (one button). Pool glyph and lead word only when the item came from a pool: **Review, Deepen, Explore, Lateral**; only Review uses `due` with the clock; no rule names. OWNER (replaces PLAN M6's pool colour). Then the sentence from 5.1: connective words in `text.secondary`, the **cause** (a concept, a topic or the learner's own action) in `text.primary`; never truncated. Tap opens the **reason panel**: 2 to 3 lines of detail, at most one extra line (priority: offline, the trigger line of 5.1, the demo clock) and, on pool reels, a "Not for me" text button.
- **Changed marker.** When the item exists because of something the learner just did (`reason.params.trigger`), the sentence rolls (`spring.fast`) and one block shows in reverse video for 1200 ms: the lead word, or without one the span naming the action ("More like this", "too easy", "Two quick skips", "wrong"). An item with neither has no marker; its reason panel line carries the cause.
- **Quick actions** (three cells, icon over label):

| Tap            | Effect on the plan                                                                                        | Visible result                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| More like this | Interest up; the next reel is re-planned at once                                                          | Strip: "Next: {concept}. Undo", or "After the question: {concept}. Undo" when a card comes first (rules 3 and 4 outrank the echo). That reel reads "Because you tapped **More like this**." with the marker. |
| Too easy       | No mastery change; one card is queued next: a prove-it card, or the concept's own review when that is due | "Prove it: 1 question next. Undo"; on a Checked concept that is not due, "... It cannot count before {countdown}." Nothing auto-advances.                                                                    |
| Not for me     | Concept hidden for the session; topic shown less from now on                                              | "Hidden this session. Less {topic}. Undo". The reel keeps playing; this impression's watch signals are ignored. The next item keeps its normal sentence; its lead word, if any, carries the marker.          |

A fired cell is marked for 1200 ms, with a light haptic. Undo lasts 4 s (10 s with a screen reader) or until the swipe, restores interest, queue and hidden state, and shows "Undone." for 2 s. The last tap wins.

### 3.3 Feed: retrieval card

Full-screen pager item, no top row: the strip names the reason and the concept. The body is a ScrollView from the status inset to the strip; the action row is pinned. **On a card the pager never follows the finger:** "Continue", "Later" and the screen-reader actions are the only exits, and there is no way back to the previous reel. That removes the body-inside-pager scroll conflict. A blocked swipe is detected on the card (a locked list emits no scroll events): a vertical drag of 24 pt or more that the body cannot scroll (`onScrollEndDrag` at the limit, or a pan responder) shows a strip message for 4 s.

| State                 | Body                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Action row                                                                        | Blocked-swipe message   |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Unanswered**        | Question, up to 5 lines. Option rows with key letters: **3 while the concept is at box 0** (first check, re-check, prove-it; one distractor dropped by the seeded draw, never the correct one; 03 fact-check), **4 from box 1**. Tap selects (haptic); another tap moves the selection. No timer. | "Later" (text button), "Check answer" (primary, disabled until a row is selected) | "Answer, or tap Later." |
| **Answered, correct** | Rows keep their place. Chosen row in the `proven` treatment with "Correct". Then verdict "Correct.", the explanation, the progress panel.                                                                                                                                                         | "Continue"                                                                        | "Tap Continue."         |
| **Answered, wrong**   | Chosen row in the `error` treatment with "Your answer". The right row gets a **neutral** outline and "Correct answer": no `proven` colour, because nothing was proven. Verdict "Not quite.", explanation ending "Another angle is next.", the panel.                                              | "Continue"; the reteach reel is next                                              | "Tap Continue."         |
| **Later**             | No mastery or interest change. Strip: "Saved for later. Back after 2 reels." The card skips cadence and the overdue guard for 2 reels, then returns with its original sentence. A card flicked past before it settles counts as Later.                                                            | -                                                                                 | -                       |

- **Select, then confirm** replaces commit-on-tap (one mis-tap could demote Checked to Seen). No Undo after a reveal. `latency_ms` runs from card shown to "Check answer".
- On "Check answer": result haptic; rows change; the body scrolls (`scrollTo`) just far enough to put the panel's bottom edge 16 above the strip; focus moves to the verdict.
- **Height from `DESIGN.md` tokens** (12 between question, options and verdict; 2-line question and options, 3-line explanation, 124 pt panel): an answered box-0 card is about 591 and a review about 667, against bodies of 591 (390x844), 580 / 556 (360x800, gesture / 3-button) and 687 (412x915). A first check just fits at 390x844 and scrolls 11 to 35 at 360x800; a review scrolls 76 to 111 at 390 and 360 wide, so after the reveal the question may leave the screen (the learner can scroll back). The authored maxima (119-character question, 80-character options, 221-character explanation) reach about 870. Layout fixtures: `prompt-injection-guardrails--example--q1`, `ts-unions-narrowing--intro--q2`, `prompt-injection-guardrails--pitfall--q2`.
- **Which item.** Items belong to reels and test that reel's claims (2 per reel). Pick from the items of this concept's reels the learner has finished (not early-skipped): least recently answered first (never answered wins), never the item on this concept's previous card; if none qualifies, the least recently answered item of any angle. Prove-it: an item from an angle other than the reel on screen, pitfall first. Items of reels missing from the manifest do not exist. So a delayed review never repeats the previous question.
- Options are shuffled per presentation by the seeded draw (the correct answer is option A in 142 of 238 authored items). A prove-it card reaches Checked at most.

### 3.4 Progress panel (after an answer only)

Inline at the bottom of the card body (`DESIGN.md`: width W - 2 x axis; 124 high, 142 with a two-line consequence). It stays until the learner leaves the card; a tap opens the map with the replay. Never after watching. Rows: the concept title; the topic's 8 tiles at 18 pt in rail order with "1/8"; the change line (old tile and state word, arrow icon, new tile and state word: "Seen", arrow, "Checked"); the consequence from 5.4, one line where it fits ("Back in about 1 min. Correct then: Learned.").

Timeline: 0 ms result and haptic; 120 ms panel in; 350 ms the tile fills (450 ms); on Learned the opened tiles turn from dash to outline 90 ms apart and a medium haptic fires once when the fill ends.

**Order** (one tested function; the matrix, the progress panel and the pins use it)

- **Rail order:** depth-first pre-order from the topic's roots, roots and children in `graph.json` node order. A concept is placed under whichever of its in-topic prerequisites is placed last, so every in-topic prerequisite sits above it; its other prerequisites become "+ needs" lines, or are implied when they are ancestors of that parent. A concept with only cross-topic prerequisites goes last at level 0. Tile n is row n.
- **Gap order:** topological; among ready steps, the selected concept's own topic first, then rail order.
- Tests: prerequisite-safe in all 5 topics; React Native = Core components, Flexbox layout, Lists and virtualization, Performance and re-renders (row 4, y 547-611 at 390x844, above the tallest dock), then the other four; Performance and re-renders pins Lists and virtualization, Components and props, State, Memoization, itself.

**Behaviour**

- **Open and select:** stage and dock open on suggestion 1. Ranking: due now, just opened, then "opens the most" (ties: topic interest, then rail order). "Opens {n}" counts the concepts that become Open if this one is Learned now (Core components 6; Tokens and context windows 3 of its 4 dependents). A tap on a rail row, a matrix tile or a "Needs" row selects that concept; the dock shows it with a "Next up" text button to go back. Selecting commits nothing.
- **Staged topic on open:** the topic of the latest level change, else the last selected, else suggestion 1's.
- **Chain marking, no dimming:** a selected locked concept gets number pins on its unmet ancestors and itself, in gap order, in rail and matrix.
- **Change topic:** a matrix row or the previous / next buttons (`color.slow` cross-fade); focus moves to the stage header. No swipe.
- **Rail specials** (38 prerequisite edges = 33 connectors + 4 "+ needs" lines + 1 implied; tested): Performance and re-renders "+ needs Memoization (React + TS)"; LLM calls as unreliable dependencies "+ needs Structured output (AI)"; Prompt injection and guardrails at level 0 with a dashed inbound stub and "+ needs Tool use and agents (AI)"; Binary-search debugging under Breaking down problems with "+ needs Reproduce before fixing" (the reverse would put it above a prerequisite); Retrieval-augmented generation under Hallucinations, its Tokens edge implied. Step counts and the jump to the other topic are in the dock detail's "Needs" rows.
- **Dock:** copy, buttons and heights per state are in 5.5; a Checked, not-due concept has **no primary button**. Only the dock countdown ticks; one timeout at the next `dueAt` flips tiles to due.
- **Dock detail:** title, topic, summary, state with box and next due time, rows for Needs / Opens / Related (11 de-duplicated pairs; tap to jump), the primary button, "Close". Covered rail rows are hidden from accessibility. **Key panel:** the seven marks with word, meaning and live count, then due mark, NEW, pin, selected, then the rule line (5.2).
- **Replay** on the first open after a level change, from any entry point (once, under 2.1 s, from a stored "last seen on the map" snapshot; a missing snapshot is the fresh-profile state): dock off-screen; old snapshot with the changed row in view; 400 ms the tile fills in matrix and rail; from 900 ms each opened child gets its connector lit, an outline and NEW, 90 ms apart; the count rolls; at 1800 ms the dock slides up retargeted. Only level changes and unlocks in the staged topic animate; changes elsewhere and new Seen dots appear unanimated and set that row's 8 pt mark (the Map tab mark still ignores watching).

### 3.6 Gap session

Same pager and players as the feed; reels keep the top row and strip. The action row has no quick actions: it shows "Item 2 of 6", the only counter on screen (the step count is in the reason panel), and a "Leave session" text button.

`gapPlan(target)` walks the unmet ancestors in gap order (3.5), then the target, and serves each step by that step's own state:

| Step's state     | Served for the step                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open or Seen     | A reel in an angle not yet watched, one more reel (another angle), then a card: 3 items. A still-locked target is served the same way. |
| Due              | One review card.                                                                                                                       |
| Checked, not due | Nothing. The session stops before this step, because a card here could not count.                                                      |

- The plan is cut to whole steps within 6 items: an Open concept is 3; a due one is one card; a locked concept with one Open ancestor is 6, ending with the check on the target; two or more unmet ancestors stop before the target.
- A wrong answer adds the reteach reel and one re-check inside the 6-item cap (PLAN M5); steps that no longer fit are dropped. The session ends on the target's first correct check, after the last planned item, or on a wrong answer with no room left.
- "Rewatch a reel" (Learned or Retained, not due): one reel, no card, no mastery change. Cut first if time is short.
- Cards work as in 3.3, except "Later" becomes "Leave session". Leaving (button or back) asks: "Leave this gap session? Your answers are kept." with "Leave" and "Stay".
- A summary item (3.7; not counted in the 6) ends a session, with "Back to feed" and "Open map". The one-card review and the rewatch skip it and return to the map.

### 3.7 Session summary and ending

A full-screen pager item, inserted after 10 items in Light or 14 in Focus (never before the first answered card), and when every unlocked reel has been shown this session. OWNER. No next item until "Keep going" is tapped.

- Title: "{a} checked, {b} learned this session." (never "proven": a first correct answer is Checked). Line: "Next review in 0:42." when one is scheduled.
- Rows: "Reels watched 9", "Questions answered 3, 2 correct", "Now Learned: Core components" (only if any).
- Buttons: "Keep going" (primary), "Open map". No streaks, targets, loss-framed copy or notifications. A cold start or a return after 30 minutes away starts a new session.

## 9. What must change in docs/PLAN.md

**Agent spec**

1. Reasons: adopt 5.1 (variants; params `trigger`, `cause`, `retry`). A box-0 concept with a retry `dueAt` is a due review for rules 3 and 4 and the Review pool.
2. **Queue re-plan** (replaces "Prefetch vs adaptivity"; mechanism in `PERFORMANCE.md` 2.5). The plan is rebuilt with the current `now` at every settle, on every card answer (right or wrong), on "Later", on a quick action or its Undo, and when the single `dueAt` timer fires. Each rebuild re-runs slot rules 1 to 4 for the item directly after the one on screen: a resulting card goes there, replacing a planned card or ahead of the loaded next reel (a card needs no player, so no preload is lost); a wrong answer puts the reteach reel there. So an unlock or a newly due review shows in the very next item. Edits wait while the list moves. A planned first check is placed only once its exposure is real (item 6). Overdue guard: "next slot" (Focus) is the item after the one on screen at `dueAt`; "within 2 slots" (Light) is no later than the second. The queue is pure code in `@reels/agent`, shared by app and tests.
3. **Random draws.** Each draw is a pure function of the seed, the reel or card ordinal and its purpose (pool, tie-break, angle, item); the option shuffle and the dropped distractor use the seed, the item id and its presentation count. A re-plan from unchanged state repeats its picks, and an inserted card, an Undo or a shuffle never moves a later reel pick.
4. Quick actions: the re-plan returns the next item at once (the confirmation names it); the echo survives an intervening card; Undo restores interest, pending prove-it, hidden flag and queue; "Not for me" voids that impression's watch signals; "Too easy" on a due concept serves the due review.
5. Cards: select-then-confirm; "Later" as a deferral state; 3 options at box 0, 4 from box 1 (OWNER); the item rule (3.3); prove-it reaches Checked at most (the exception to "first check at least one reel after exposure"); a reteach reel may replay when no unplayed angle is left.
6. Exposure: Seen, and eligible for a first check, after a reel end that is not an early skip. Leaving the feed pauses an impression (section 2). One rewatch signal per impression; playback pauses after 2 idle loops.
7. No-repeat filter: state it exactly; the script assumes "not among the previous 2 reels". OWNER.
8. Honesty gap: a Checked concept still qualifies for Deepen, so its reel can play right before its own review and "delayed" recall becomes recognition. Recommended: keep a concept out of the reel pools while its review is due or due within 90 s (reteach excepted). That covers the 60 s demo interval and, on the real clock, only the last 90 s before the card; excluding every concept with a scheduled review would empty the real-clock pools (6 roots Checked after about 12 reels, the first review minutes away). Run `npm run simulate` on both clocks and modes before deciding. OWNER.
9. Demo clock: sets Focus, stores the previous mode, rescales pending `dueAt` (section 2). Default mode Light. "Skip": prior 0.5. Summary item per 3.7.
10. Gap session: `gapPlan` per 3.6 (steps by state, 6-item cap, wrong-answer rule, one-card review); confirm a session may serve a still-locked target.
11. Display states: add Locked and Open; `displayState(concept, profile, now)` is pure, with just-opened and due flags; a `map_seen` snapshot sits beside mastery. Sample profiles (3.8) are fixtures; profiles created under the demo clock get the demo seed; Reset starts a new session; `graph.json` gains short topic names.
