## Users

- **Primary (PLAN):** working developers, junior to mid level; content is English only. The five topics **(repo)**: React Native, React and TypeScript, Problem solving, AI engineering, Systems design in the age of AI.
- **Situation (inferred):** short gaps in the day, phone in one hand, a few minutes at a time, often with sound off. They already know the short-video swipe.
- **Job (inferred):** show me 30-second pieces on what I pick, open more as I prove the basics, and tell me what I can still recall later.
- **Second audience (PLAN M7):** people watching a 3-minute demo on a real phone, and three testers who install it unaided. They must see the feed adapt and the map change on screen, without a tap, while a presenter follows the demo script (r03, r09).

## Product Purpose

- **What it is (PLAN, repo):** a demo (no accounts, pricing, store listing or notifications) of a vertical feed of pre-rendered lesson reels, 24 to 37 s each, over one curriculum, "Software engineering in the age of AI": 5 topics, 40 concepts, 38 prerequisite links, 11 related pairs.
- **Why it exists (r03):** learners who restudied beat learners who were tested at 5 minutes (81% vs 75%) and fell behind at 1 week (42% vs 56%); in the same paper's second experiment the study-only group rated its own future recall highest (Roediger & Karpicke 2006: students reading prose passages). Nothing tests 30 s reels or working developers. The app therefore counts watching as exposure only.
- **Success (PLAN):** a fresh profile on a real phone sees, in about 3 minutes and in this order: a swipe, the feed adapts and says why, a first check, the same concept returns as a delayed review, the map shows it Learned. In the demo, Learned is reached on the demo clock; the reason panel and the You tab say that the clock is on (UX.md section 2). Then three testers install unaided, and their exported logs answer PLAN's two questions: reels and questions per session with the early-skip rate ("Keep going" taps counted separately), and delayed-review accuracy.

## Positioning

Closest neighbours: Execute Program (for developers; prerequisites unlock only after a delayed review; its display tops out at Learned; r01, from an undated note) and ScrollEd and 5Mins.ai (quizzes in a swipe feed; r03 knows them from press coverage, neither app was opened, so their scheduling is unknown). What this demo combines: an interest-steered short-video feed, delayed-answer gating, a stated reason on every item, and Retained shown above Learned so the map does not look finished after one review. **Unverified:** that no neighbour does all four. **Open task:** open ScrollEd and 5Mins.ai before the question pattern is final (r03).

1. **Two records with separate inputs (PLAN).** Interest starts from the onboarding pick, then moves only from watch-through, one rewatch per impression (the r03 cap), early skip, More like this and Not for me. Mastery moves only from answers. Too easy moves neither record; it queues one question. The agent reads both records to choose the next item.
2. **Learned needs a delayed answer (PLAN).** A first correct answer makes a concept Checked. Only a correct answer on or after its due time makes it Learned, and only Learned concepts unlock what depends on them.
3. **A true reason on every item (PLAN).** A rule-based agent picks each reel and question, so the app can state the rule and cause that actually fired.
4. **A map of all 40 concepts (PLAN, r09).** It shows every concept and its prerequisites; watching and answering leave different marks.

## Operating Context

- **Feed (r03, brief):** phone, portrait. Vertical swipe between full-screen items; reels loop, never auto-advance, tap to pause. At most one rewatch counts per impression and playback pauses after two idle loops (not in PLAN, where every extra loop of 50% or more raises interest). The video is shown whole (contain-fit) and app chrome sits outside the picture where the phone is taller than 9:16.
- **Sound (owner decision, 2026-10-04):** reels play with sound on by default; a visible, labelled Sound control turns it off and the choice persists. Captions still carry the lesson for anyone who turns sound off.
- **Content (PLAN):** made offline by a writer model, four review gates, TTS narration and a Remotion render; then the owner approves or rejects each reel. Only approved reels reach the app, from a static host.
- **Modes (PLAN):** Light (a question every 4 reels; draw weights Review 0.15, Deepen 0.25) and Focus (every 2 reels; Review 0.35, Deepen 0.45). The demo runs in Focus with the demo clock on (first review due 60 s after a correct answer instead of 10 min); Light cannot close the loop in 3 minutes (r03). First launch is Light; turning the demo clock on also switches to Focus, and turning it off restores the earlier mode (UX.md section 2; open). While it is on, the reason panel adds "Demo clock: reviews return in about 1 min." There is no persistent label in the feed or on the map: a band there would take 24 pt from the map during the demo.
- **Profile (PLAN):** no account. Onboarding is one screen: pick any of the 5 topics, or skip, under the contract "Watching marks a concept Seen. Only a correct answer after a delay marks it Learned. Your swipes and three buttons steer the feed." (skip and contract: r03) Profile and event log stay on the phone; the log exports through the share sheet. Settings hold Light / Focus, the demo clock, sound, haptics, three sample profiles, the log export and "Reset progress" (PLAN M5 to M7); they sit in the You tab (UX.md 3.8).

## Brand Commitments

- **Personality (inferred):** it reports the learner's state in plain words. It does not cheer, nag or sell.
- **Voice (inferred):** plain sentences in sentence case, second person. A reason says what the item is, then why it is here; a confirmation says what will happen, then when. Exact names, counts and times wherever the agent has them; never "soon" or "a few". "About" is allowed only for when a review comes back: its due time is exact, the item it lands after is not. Time formats (UX.md section 5): elapsed "45s", "1m 06s", "9m", "2d"; countdown "0:42"; counts "1/40". One noun per thing on screen: "question" for the unit, "check" for a first one, "review" for a delayed one; never "card" on screen (the specs call the component a retrieval card). Verdicts are flat ("Correct.", "Not quite."): no praise, exclamation marks or emoji. A button names the action and, for a locked target, where it starts ("Start with Lists and virtualization"); the line above it gives the size ("3 items: a reel, one more, a question.").
- **Evidence words are never upgraded (brief, r03 fact-check):** "answered" for a Checked concept, "learned" only after a delayed correct answer. Slot-rule names (echo, reteach, change of pace, bridge, fallback) never reach the screen; the sentence says what happened. The learner-facing terms are fixed in UX.md: the four pool words (open, see the Pool row), "Prove it" and "gap session". Every duration in a string comes from the active interval table.

## Product Principles

1. **Honest mastery (brief).** Watching earns one neutral Seen mark and a Seen count that is never added to answered counts. It earns nothing an answer earns: no answered-state mark, haptic, panel or headline number. Only answers move a concept further; only a correct delayed answer makes it Learned.
2. **Visible reasons (PLAN).** Every reel and question says why it is here, in one plain sentence taken from the rule that fired. Never a reason the agent did not use.
3. **Learner control (inferred).** Every quick action is applied before the next item is chosen, and its confirmation says what will happen and when. Every gesture has a visible, labelled control; the one exception, to be checked with the three testers, is tap-to-pause on a playing reel, where only the paused state shows a control (UX.md section 10). A question can be put off with "Later" but never skipped by a stray swipe.
4. **Sessions that end (r03).** No auto-advance, streaks or loss framing, and no notifications in the demo (reminders for due reviews are undecided). After a set number of items the feed stops on a summary, and going on is a choice.
5. **One vocabulary (inferred).** Locked and Open, Seen to Retained, and Due mean the same thing in the feed, on questions and on the map.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Colour (r03, r09):** no state by colour alone; every state also has a shape, icon or word. Contrast is computed, not judged by eye: text at least 4.5:1, controls and glyphs at least 3:1. Still to do: greyscale and colour-blindness checks on a phone, and an increased-contrast variant that follows the system contrast setting (r09, ergonomics must-fix).
- **Input (r03, r09):** no gesture-only action (no long-press or double-tap functions). Buttons at least 48 x 48 pt (iOS) / dp (Android), 8 apart; list rows at least 48 high, and they may touch. Two exceptions, both in DESIGN.md: on iOS the feed top row, the map header and the legend row are 44 pt high (48 on Android); and the map's small tiles and 42 to 44 pt matrix rows, each with a full-size rail row on the same screen.
- **Time and motion (r03):** no time limit on any question. Reels can be paused and sound has a visible control. Reduce Motion is honoured for programmatic motion (the finger-tracked swipe stays); reels still autoplay, because the re-rendered reels only fade (open decision 9). With a screen reader running, reels wait for Play. No meaning depends on a haptic.
- **Text (r03, r09):** questions scale to 200% by scrolling; the map's list and start area to at least 1.6x, with a large-text layout from 1.35x; feed chrome is capped at 1.3x (quick-action, tab and Sound labels at 1.2x; DESIGN.md). Baked reel text cannot scale, so every reel needs a native transcript holding narration, on-screen text and code, in the concept panel and behind a screen-reader "Read transcript" action (required; UX.md 6.1 and 6.2). The manifest has only caption words today (repo); adding a plain `transcript`, `on_screen_text` and code from the reel spec is a schema change.
