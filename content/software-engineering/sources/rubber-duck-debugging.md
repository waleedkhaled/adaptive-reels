# Rubber duck debugging

1. Rubber duck debugging means explaining your code, line by line, out loud to someone or something, even a rubber duck.
2. The name comes from the book The Pragmatic Programmer, where a programmer explains code to a rubber duck.
3. Explaining forces you to say what each line actually does instead of what you assume it does, which often reveals the bug.
4. Writing a clear question for a colleague works the same way, and the answer often appears before you send it.
5. It works best when you explain from the start, including the parts you are sure are correct.
